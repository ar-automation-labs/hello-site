The Expensive Bottleneck

Drowning Under 100+ Daily Unread Emails

Business executives spend the first 90 minutes of their morning wading through routine status inquiries, vendor pitches, receipts, and newsletters. In the chaos, high-priority client requests and urgent contract revisions sit buried until it's too late.

Handing over your inbox to a human VA is expensive and raises confidentiality risks. Fully automated email bots that send replies directly can cause catastrophic mistakes. What's needed is a safe, draft-only copilot.

The Solution Delivered

Smart Triage with 100% Draft-Only Safety Guardrails

1
Multi-Label Triage: Inbound threads are evaluated and automatically labeled by priority: 🔥 Urgent Client, 💬 Routine Inquiry, 📰 Newsletter, or 🛑 Cold Pitch.
2
Contextual Draft Generation: For actionable emails, Claude analyzes the full thread history and crafts a tailored, professional response saved directly to your Gmail Drafts folder.
3
Zero Unsolicited Sends: The system NEVER sends an email on its own. You simply open Drafts, review in 5 seconds, tweak if needed, and hit send.
4
Morning Executive Digest: At 8:00 AM, a bulleted digest is sent to your phone summarizing the top 3 items requiring your personal decision today.
