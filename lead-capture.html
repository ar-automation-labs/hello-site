AI-Powered Lead Qualification & Appointment System

Capture. Qualify. Respond. Book. Track. Automatically. Built by Ali Raza, founder of AR Automation Labs.

Built with:
n8nGmail APIGoogle SheetsOpenAITally FormsCalendlySupabaseTelegram

Most businesses lose leads simply because nobody responds fast enough. This system captures every inbound lead the moment it arrives, scores it as HOT, WARM, COLD, or BAD, replies within seconds with a personalized message, and sends you an instant alert for hot leads. Interested leads automatically get a Calendly link so they can book a call themselves — and everything is tracked live on a dashboard, so you always know exactly where each lead stands.

What you get
✓Never miss a lead — every inquiry gets a response within seconds, day or night
✓Know who to call first — leads are automatically scored by urgency
✓Fewer no-shows — interested leads book their own call slot
✓One clean dashboard — see every lead's status without digging through emails
✓Stops nagging automatically — follow-ups stop the moment someone replies or books
Who it's for

Built for anyone whose business lives or dies on response speed. Real estate agents use it to qualify buyers the moment an inquiry lands, before another agent gets there first. Coaches and marketing agencies use it to sort serious prospects from browsers automatically, so no discovery call gets booked with the wrong person. If leads come through a website form, DMs, or ads — and losing even one to a slow reply costs you money — this is built for that.
