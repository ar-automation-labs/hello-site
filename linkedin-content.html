Project

LinkedIn Content Automation

Turns a simple idea list into finished LinkedIn posts, built by Ali Raza at AR Automation Labs.

Built with:
n8nOpenAIDALL·ELinkedIn APIGoogle Sheets

Knowing you should post on LinkedIn and actually finding the time are two different things. This system takes a simple spreadsheet of post ideas, researches each topic, writes the post copy, generates a matching image, and waits for your one-click approval before it goes live. Nothing publishes without you seeing it first.

What you get
✓Stay consistent — posts go out regularly without you writing every one
✓Saves real time — research and writing happen automatically
✓Always on-brand — you approve every post before it's published
✓Built-in visuals — a relevant image is generated for each post
Who it's for

Built especially for consultants and coaches whose next client usually comes from LinkedIn, not ads — where staying visible matters more than posting perfectly. Also a good fit for founders and marketing agencies who want a steady content presence without a dedicated content team.
