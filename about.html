About Ali Raza

Founder of AR Automation Labs — AI automation specialist, based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, working with businesses worldwide.

Ali Raza is the founder of AR Automation Labs, an AI automation studio building practical, custom-built systems for small businesses — not templated, one-size-fits-all bots. Based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, Ali works with clients internationally, from real estate agents in the US to coaches and consultants across different time zones.

The work centers on n8n as the core automation engine, connected to tools like OpenAI, Claude, Pinecone, Supabase, Gmail, and LinkedIn's API to build systems that capture leads, run AI chatbots, manage inboxes, and keep content flowing — all summarized on the projects section of the homepage.

People sometimes search for this work under different names — Ali Raza automation, AR Automation Labs, or Ali Automation — it's all the same person and the same studio. If you found this page searching for any of those, you're in the right place.

Outside of specific project work, Ali focuses on making automation genuinely usable for non-technical business owners: plug-and-play systems, clear documentation, and full ownership handed to the client at the end — no ongoing lock-in required.
